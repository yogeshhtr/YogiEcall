# Yogi Ecall 📞

<img src="icon_preview.png" width="120" align="right" />

**Calling made easy for elders: no searching for names, just tap the photo.** No ads, no account.

* **Big photos of family**, 3 in a row like a keypad. You can choose 1 to 4 in a row, and 4 name sizes.
* **Tap a photo to call.** By default a **3-second countdown** shows the photo and *"Calling Aama..."* with a big Cancel, so a wrong tap is easy to undo. The phone also **says the name aloud**. Or choose *Ask first* or *Call at once*.
* **Hold a photo** for WhatsApp, a text message, or the number.
* **Emergency buttons**: Police 100, Ambulance 102, Fire 101. Each asks before calling.
* A big **clock, date and battery** at the top.
* **Setup is for the family**: hold the gear button for two seconds, so it can't be changed by accident. Then:
  * add people from the phone's contacts (with their photo), or type a name and number
  * choose a photo, pick a colour, move people around, or remove them
* **Can be the phone's home screen**, so the phone always opens on these photos.
